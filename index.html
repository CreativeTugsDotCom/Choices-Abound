Ready to schedule? Request an Appointment →

Yes. Choices Abound Counseling Services offers both in-person appointments at our Darlington, SC office (520 S Main St, Darlington, SC 29532) and secure, HIPAA-compliant telehealth sessions for clients located in South Carolina and North Carolina.

You can request an appointment directly through our secure online client portal at any time, or call 843-992-7363 to speak about availability and next steps. Once requested, you’ll receive intake forms electronically before your first visit.

Choices Abound accepts insurance plans including Aetna, Ambetter, UMR, and Absolute Total Care South Carolina, as well as secure online payments. Because benefits vary by individual policy, please confirm your specific coverage and copay details prior to your first session.

Your first session is a welcoming, unhurried conversation focused on getting to know you, understanding what brought you to counseling, and discussing your hopes and goals. Together, we’ll create a thoughtful path forward tailored to your needs and pace.

Alethia integrates evidence-based and compassionate approaches—including Grief Counseling, Cognitive Behavioral Therapy (CBT), Compassion-Focused Therapy, Culturally Sensitive Care, Narrative Therapy, and Family/Marital Therapy—adapting every session to the individual or couple in front of her.
